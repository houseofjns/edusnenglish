Section C: Unseen Reading Comprehension (Total: 20 Marks)
The sun had barely risen when Mia stepped out of the tent. A chilly breeze swept across the mountain ridge, tugging at her jacket. Below, a sea of clouds rolled gently over the valleys, making the peaks look like islands in a white ocean. It was her first time hiking so high, and the sight took her breath away.

She turned to find her older brother, Leo, already boiling water over a small camp stove. "Morning," he said, handing her a steaming mug of cocoa. They sat together in silence, watching the golden light spill across the horizon. It was moments like these that made the difficult climb worthwhile.

As they packed up their campsite, Mia noticed something shiny in the grass. It was an old compass, its glass cracked but the needle still moving. She picked it up and showed it to Leo. "Someone must have lost this years ago," she said.

Leo examined it with curiosity. "Maybe it belonged to another hiker, or even a ranger," he guessed. "It's kind of cool, actually."

They decided to keep the compass as a memory of their adventure. As they descended the trail, Mia thought about how nature had a way of reminding people of both the past and the present—of old paths and new beginnings. The climb had been hard, but the journey had taught her more than she ever expected.
1. What was the setting of the passage, and how is it described?
2. What can we infer about Mia's feelings towards the hike?
3. What does the compass symbolize in the story?
4. Find a word from the passage that means 'view from a high place'.
5. What is the main message or theme of this passage?
